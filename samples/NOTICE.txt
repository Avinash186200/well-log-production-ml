force2020_wells.csv is derived from:

Source: Bormann, Peter; Aursand, Peder; Dilib, Fahad; Manral, Surrender; Dischington, Peter (2020).
  "FORCE 2020 Well well log and lithofacies dataset for machine learning competition" (v1).
  Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4351156
Licence: CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Underlying data: contains data under the Norwegian licence for Open Government data (NLOD) 2.0,
  https://data.norge.no/nlod/en/2.0, distributed by the Norwegian government (the Zenodo record gives the licence as "NOLD 2.0" and does not name the agency).

Changes made: each LAS file was parsed, curves were mapped to canonical names, values outside
plausible ranges were counted, and every well was reduced to one row of summary statistics
(coverage, units, spike counts, medians, location in km, lithology counts). No depth samples
are included. Rebuild with `make force` (downloads the original zip and checks its MD5 and
SHA-256).
